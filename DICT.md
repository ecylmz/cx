# Dictionary

Terms for accounts and subscriptions in cx.

## Terms

**Account**
A sign-in that cx manages. Each account belongs to one subscription.

**Provider**
The service that authenticates an account and reports its quota, such as Codex or Antigravity.

**Subscription**
The combination of one provider and one tier, such as Codex Plus. The dashboard groups accounts by subscription.

**Tier**
The access level within a provider, such as Plus, Pro 100, or Ultra 200.
